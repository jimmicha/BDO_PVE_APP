# Reviewed progression catalog

The initial PC NA/EU version was reviewed on 2026-09-07 and requires another review by 2026-10-07. It models the Olvia Academy prerequisite followed by six separate family claims: necklace, belt, ring I, ring II, earring I and earring II. Each reward requires 10 Essence of Dawn and 50 Sharp Black Crystal Shards. Existing claims cannot be duplicated by reopening a step or creating another goal.

- [Quest reference](https://bdocodex.com/us/quest/2308/1/).

The catalog does not promise that enrollment windows remain open. Confirm eligibility and current quest text in game. Unknown prices stay unknown; unverified mechanics do not drive recommendations.
